## What you get

- Live picture from the forward camera
- Cars in the oncoming lane marked yellow
- Your speed from the OBDLink CX, with the key on
- Playback of a take, with a yellow speed plate when a speed was saved
- A file next to the video: your speed, throttle, how hard you slowed, and each oncoming car

The number on a yellow car is an estimate. It is how fast the gap is closing, minus your speed. It is not a radar reading.

## Install

1. Open the downloaded `VELA.apk` on the phone or tablet.
2. If Android asks, allow install from Files.
3. Open VELA. Allow the camera, the microphone, and nearby devices so the plug can connect.
4. Mount it landscape, square to the hood. Line up the **lens**, not the middle of the tablet. On a Kindle the lens sits off to one side.
5. Key on. The speed chip should show miles per hour, not NO CX.
6. Hit REC while you drive. PLAY opens the take.

This build is set for a lens 4 feet up, square, on the dash of a 2022 Taos.

## Limits

A Kindle camera will not see as far as a phone camera. A speed only appears when the other car stays in view for about half a second, the tires are on the road in the picture, and the plug is connected. A car that only flashes the edge does not get a number.
